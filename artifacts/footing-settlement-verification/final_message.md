2. **Findings and Supporting Working**:
   - **Finding 1 — Omission of Primary Consolidation in `CB-GEO-007`**: Although the EOR transcribed all Stratum 2 clay consolidation parameters onto `CB-GEO-007` Sheet 2 (`INPUT`) and Sheet 5 (`Inputs`), **Sheet 4 (`Consolidation`) was left completely blank (`"—"`)** with the note *"Not computed.    - **Finding 2 — Primary Consolidation ($S_c$) and Total Settlement ($S_{\text{total}}$) Exceed the 1.0-in Tolerance by 5.1× to 6.3×**:
     With $\frac{C_c H}{1 + e_0} = \frac{0.28 \times 216\text{ in}}{1.92} = 31.500\text{ in}$ ($2.625\text{ ft}$) and $\frac{C_r H}{1 + e_0} = \frac{0.045 \times 216\text{ in}}{1.92} = 5.0625\text{ in}$:
     - *Method A (GIR §8.1 normally-consolidated formula from $\sigma'_0 = 1,850\text{ psf}$ to $\sigma'_f = 2,750\text{ psf}$)*:
       $$S_c = 31.500\text{ in} \times \log_{10}\left(\frac{1,850 + 900}{1,850}\right) = 31.500 \times 0.172161 = \mathbf{5.42\text{ in}}\ (0.452\text{ ft})$$
     - *Method B (Two-branch bi-linear recompression from $1,850 \rightarrow 1,900\text{ psf}$ + virgin compression from $1,900 \rightarrow 2,750\text{ psf}$)*:
       $$S_c = 5.0625\log_{10}\left(\frac{1,900}{1,850}\right) + 31.500\log_{10}\left(\frac{2,750}{1,900}\right) = 0.059 + 5.058 = \mathbf{5.12\text{ in}}\ (0.426\text{ ft})$$
     - *Method C (Single-branch virgin compression referenced to $\sigma'_p = 1,900\text{ psf}$, $\sigma'_f = 2,800\text{ psf}$)*:
       $$S_c = 31.500\text{ in} \times \log_{10}\left(\frac{1,900 + 900}{1,900}\right) = 31.500 \times 0.168404 = \mathbf{5.30\text{ in}}\ (0.442\text{ ft})$$
     - Combined with the EOR's immediate settlement $S_i = 0.90\text{ in}$ at governing interior footing F-C6 ($0.82\text{ in}$ at F-C7), total long-term settlement reaches **$S_{\text{total}} = S_i + S_c = 6.02\text{ to }6.32\text{ in}$** at F-C6 (**$5.94\text{ to }6.24\text{ in}$** at F-C7), exceeding the **$1.0\text{ in}$** maximum total settlement limit by more than sixfold.
