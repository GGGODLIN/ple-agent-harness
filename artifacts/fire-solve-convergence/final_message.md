### Summary of Key Results & Engineering Disposition

#### 1. Disposition on Steady-Solve Attempt 3, Residual Plateau Diagnosis & Steady-Solution Existence
- **Formal Disposition (`10/10/2025`):** Steady-solve Attempt 3 (`/Projects/CFDME-2025-041_Ocotillo_WUI/Case/logs/log.solve_20251009_attempt3.txt`, SLURM Job `41304` on `borrego`, executed `10/09/2025` by Sartaj Bhatia, Mulshi Simulation Labs) is **rejected**, and Mulshi is prohibited from launching a fourth steady-solve attempt. All three operator recommendations (reducing velocity under-relaxation from $0.30\text{ (dimensionless)}$ to $0.20\text{ (dimensionless)}$, tightening pressure tolerance to $1.00\times 10^{-8}\text{ (dimensionless)}$ with extra GAMG smoother sweeps, or loosening outer residual controls to $1.00\times 10^{-3}\text{ (dimensionless)}$ on $U_x$ and $1.00\times 10^{-4}\text{ (dimensionless)}$ on $p$ to accept a cycle-averaged RANS field) are disapproved.
- **What the Residual Plateau Is:** After monotonic residual decay across outer iterations $1\text{ iteration}$ to $400\text{ iterations}$, initial residuals enter a periodic oscillation across iterations $400\text{ iterations}$ to $800\text{ iterations}$ in the band $3.24\times 10^{-4}$ to $7.83\times 10^{-4}\text{ (dimensionless)}$ ($3.00\times 10^{-4}$ to $8.00\times 10^{-4}\text{ dimensionless}$) on $U_x$ and $2.02\times 10^{-5}$ to $2.86\times 10^{-5}\text{ (dimensionless)}$ ($2.00\times 10^{-5}$ to $3.00\times 10^{-5}\text{ dimensionless}$) on pressure $p$. This is **not** numerical convergence stalling or pressure-solver noise; it is the **physical buoyancy-driven puffing instability (periodic necking, toroidal vortex shedding, and plume re-formation) of the $30.1\text{ MW}$ fire plume** trapped in a pseudo-time outer-SIMPLE limit cycle.
- **Theoretical vs. Observed Frequency & Period and Percentage Closeness:**
  - **Theoretical Plume Values (`CFDME_Modeling_Standards_Fire_rev4.pdf` §4, Cetegen–Ahmed correlation for $D = 3.00\text{ m}$):**
    - Frequency: $f = 1.5 \cdot (3.00\text{ m})^{-1/2} = \mathbf{0.87\text{ Hz}}$ (unrounded analytical: $0.8660\text{ Hz}$).
    - Period: $T = 1/f = \mathbf{1.15\text{ s}}$ (unrounded analytical: $1.1547\text{ s}$ from $1/0.866025\text{ Hz}$, or $1.1494\text{ s}$ from $1/0.87\text{ Hz}$).
  - **What Attempt 3 Showed (`log.solve_20251009_attempt3.txt`):**
    - Observed Period: **$T_{\text{run}} = 1.15\text{ s}$** from autocorrelation of the last $200\text{ residual samples}$ ($\sim 1150\text{ outer iterations}$ of internal pseudo-time) and synchronized oscillation at plume-centreline Probe 0 ($x = 1.50\text{ m}, y = 0.00\text{ m}, z = 8.00\text{ m}$, oscillating by $\sim 40.0\text{ K}$ peak-to-peak in $T$ and $\sim 2.40\text{ m/s}$ peak-to-peak in $U_z$).
    - Observed Frequency: **$f_{\text{run}} = 1 / 1.15\text{ s} = 0.87\text{ Hz}$** (unrounded: $0.8696\text{ Hz}$).
  - **Percentage Closeness:**
    - At two-decimal reporting precision ($0.87\text{ Hz}$ and $1.15\text{ s}$): **$0.00\text{ \%}$ discrepancy ($100.00\text{ \%}$ agreement)** on both frequency and period.
    - Against unrounded analytical values ($f = 0.8660\text{ Hz}$, $T = 1.1547\text{ s}$): **$0.41\text{ \%}$ discrepancy ($99.59\text{ \%}$ agreement)** on both period ($|1.15\text{ s} - 1.1547\text{ s}| / 1.1547\text{ s} = 0.41\text{ \%}$) and frequency ($|0.8696\text{ Hz} - 0.8660\text{ Hz}| / 0.8660\text{ Hz} = 0.41\text{ \%}$), and **$0.05\text{ \%}$ discrepancy ($99.95\text{ \%}$ agreement)** comparing unrounded $f_{\text{run}} = 0.8696\text{ Hz}$ against $0.87\text{ Hz}$.
- **Existence of a Steady Solution:** **No steady-state (time-invariant) solution exists.** Across the SOW §3.1 wind speeds ($5.00\text{ m/s}$, $8.00\text{ m/s}$, $12.00\text{ m/s}$ at $6.10\text{ m}$ reference height over $D_{\text{char}} = 3.00\text{ m}$), the Froude number $\text{Fr} = u_{\text{wind}} / \sqrt{g \cdot D_{\text{char}}}$ spans $0.92$, $1.48$, and $2.21\text{ (dimensionless)}$ (mixed convection). Per Modeling Standard rev 4 §4, because the plume oscillates at a finite, deterministic physical frequency, no time-invariant flow field exists that any residual-tolerance schedule can converge to.
