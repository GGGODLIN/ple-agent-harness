### 3. Whether the Resolved-Energy Figure Was Taken Inside the Index Region & Effect of Site Conditions on $D^*$
* **Was the $84\%$ resolved-energy figure taken inside the region the index describes?**

* **Do the ambient values behind $D^*$ represent the site, and what would site conditions do to it?**
  * **No:** $\rho_\infty = 1.204\text{ kg/m}^3$, $T_\infty = 293.15\text{ K}$ ($20.0\,^\circ\text{C}$), and $c_p = 1005\text{ J/(kg}\cdot\text{K)}$ are standard $20.0\,^\circ\text{C}$ sea-level reference properties ($101.32\text{ kPa}$), not the Avra Valley Sonoran Desert WUI site envelope ($T_\infty = 35\text{--}42\,^\circ\text{C}$ [$308.15\text{--}315.15\text{ K}$], RH $10\text{--}25\%$, elevation $\sim 750\text{ m}$ with $K_e \approx 0.92$ / $p_{\text{site}} \approx 92.6\text{--}93.2\text{ kPa}$, and nominal site density $1.2\text{ kg/m}^3$ in `windload_basis_memo.pdf` §2.1a & §5; nor the non-compliant Dry Wash Aerospace high-altitude values $\rho_\infty = 0.4\text{ kg/m}^3, T_\infty = 223\text{ K}, p_\infty = 26{,}500\text{ Pa}$ archived in `OAS_case_setup_from_DWA.txt`, which would yield $D^* = 6.48\text{ m}$ and $D^*/0.5\text{ m} = 12.97$).
  * **Effect of Avra Valley site conditions on $D^*$ and $D^*/dx$:**
    1. **Updating $T_\infty$ to $35\text{--}42\,^\circ\text{C}$ ($308.15\text{--}315.15\text{ K}$) at fixed $\rho_\infty = 1.204\text{ kg/m}^3$ (or $1.20\text{ kg/m}^3$):** Since $D^* \propto T_\infty^{-2/5}$, $D^*$ **decreases by $1.97\%$ to $2.85\%$**, from $3.76\text{ m}$ ($3.742\text{ m}$ exact) to **$3.67\text{--}3.69\text{ m}$** at $35\,^\circ\text{C}$ ($3.668\text{ m}$ exact / $3.686\text{ m}$ scaled; $3.673\text{ m}$ at $\rho_\infty = 1.20\text{ kg/m}^3$) and **$3.64\text{--}3.65\text{ m}$** at $42\,^\circ\text{C}$ ($3.636\text{ m}$ exact / $3.653\text{ m}$ scaled; $3.640\text{ m}$ at $\rho_\infty = 1.20\text{ kg/m}^3$), lowering $D^*/dx$ (at $dx = 0.5\text{ m}$) to **$7.27\text{--}7.38$**.
    2. **Consistent ideal-gas adjustment at constant sea-level pressure ($101.32\text{ kPa}$):** Heating air to $35\text{--}42\,^\circ\text{C}$ lowers $\rho_\infty$ proportionally to **$1.145\text{--}1.120\text{ kg/m}^3$**, keeping the denominator product $\rho_\infty T_\infty = p_\infty / R_{\text{air}} = 352.95\text{ kg}\cdot\text{K/m}^3$ invariant and leaving **$D^* = 3.742\text{ m}$ ($3.76\text{ m}$ reported) and $D^*/dx = 7.48\text{--}7.52$ unchanged**.
    3. **Consistent ideal-gas adjustment including the $\sim 750\text{ m}$ Avra Valley elevation ($p_{\text{site}} \approx 92.6\text{--}93.2\text{ kPa}, K_e \approx 0.92$) and $T_\infty = 35\text{--}42\,^\circ\text{C}$:** Lower barometric pressure reduces $\rho_\infty$ to **$1.047\text{--}1.054\text{ kg/m}^3$** (at $35\,^\circ\text{C}$) and **$1.024\text{--}1.030\text{ kg/m}^3$** (at $42\,^\circ\text{C}$), decreasing $\rho_\infty T_\infty$ by $8.0\text{--}8.6\%$ and **increasing $D^*$ by $+3.39\%$ to $+3.65\%$** to **$3.87\text{--}3.90\text{ m}$** ($3.869\text{--}3.879\text{ m}$ exact / $3.888\text{--}3.897\text{ m}$ scaled), which raises $D^*/dx$ (at $dx = 0.5\text{ m}$) to **$7.74\text{--}7.79$**.
    * **Net result:** Under all site-condition formulations, $D^*/dx$ at $dx = 0.5\text{ m}$ stays within **$7.27\text{ to }7.79$**, well inside the firm’s $[5, 10]$ acceptance band.
