     1. *Prohibited Overnight Minimum Load Applied:* Memo `v1` identified a feeder minimum load of **`1.1 MW` (`1,100 kW`)** on Feeder `ALN-214`. Using the overnight minimum directly violated `SRP-3 Rev 3 §4` (effective `02/10/2025`), which mandates: *"For solar DER, the minimum load used in this screen shall be the minimum load within the daytime window 10:00–16:00, determined from feeder AMI interval data. The absolute overnight minimum shall NOT be used. Requested DER capacity must be ≤ this daytime-window minimum for the site to pass the screen."*
     2. *Misstated Data Window, Interval Cadence, and Omitted Interval Count:* Memo `v1` cited a rolling data window of `06/24/2024 – 06/24/2025` at a `Sixty (60) minutes` cadence and omitted the count of intervals evaluated required by `SRP-3 Rev 3 §6(c)`.

     - **Interval Collection Cadence (`SRP-3 Rev 3 §4`):** **`Fifteen (15) minutes`** (`96` intervals/day; **`35,040` total 15-minute intervals**).
     - **Count of Daytime (`10:00–16:00`) Intervals Evaluated (`SRP-3 Rev 3 §6(c)`):** **`8,760` intervals** with start times in `10:00–15:45` (`24 intervals/day × 365 days`), or **`9,125` intervals** with start times in `10:00–16:00` inclusive (`25 intervals/day × 365 days`) [and `10,220` intervals across hours `10:00–16:45`].
