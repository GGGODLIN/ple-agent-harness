#### 4. Where Does the Overflow End Up Once It Leaves the East Berm?
- **Committed Disposition**: Once emergency overflow leaves the east berm weir crest (`2,858.50 ft` NAVD 88), it follows a four-stage, fully contained surface pathway that bypasses all residential lots (`Lots 31–60`):
  1. **Down the `3H:1V` Armored Exterior Chute to the Horizontal Apron at Natural Grade (`2,858.50 ft` to `2,853.00 ft` NAVD 88)**: Flow descends the `5.50 ft` vertical drop (`16.50 ft` horizontal run / `17.392527130926087 ft` slope length) down the `3H:1V` grouted rip-rap exterior berm slope and traverses the `15.00 ft × 60.0 ft` horizontal grouted rip-rap energy-dissipation apron (`D50 = 12 in.`) to natural grade beyond the east berm at `2,853.00 ft` NAVD 88 (`DSR_Ph3_Improvement_Plans_C1-C9.pdf`, Sheet C-6 Page 12 & Sheet C-9 Page 17).
