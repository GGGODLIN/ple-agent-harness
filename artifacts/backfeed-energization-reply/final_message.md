### 3. What Stands in the Way, What Each Item Rests On, and Required Actions Before 07/29/2025

1. **Pending Kelloway Township Final Electrical Inspection & Missing Certificate of Final Approval (`Permit EP-25-0418`; `CX-HB-03`)**
   - **What stands in the way:** Final electrical inspection under Permit `EP-25-0418` is only **booked for 07/24/2025**; no Certificate of Final Approval has been issued.
   - **What it rests on:** Kelloway Township Electrical Permit **`EP-25-0418`** (issued `05/12/2025` by **Kelloway Township Building Department (Graham Telford, Electrical Inspector)** under adopted code **NFPA 70, National Electrical Code, 2023 edition (Kelloway Township Ordinance 2024-03)**) covers the entire installation (`"four inverter skids, the ac collection system, a 2,500 kVA pad-mounted step-up transformer and its 13.2 kV interconnection"`) and prohibits energizing on its face: **"No permitted work shall be energized before final approval."**
   - **What must be done and by whom:** After correcting the 2023 NEC deficiencies below, **Tamsworth Electrical Constructors Inc.** must pass the `07/24/2025` final inspection with **Graham Telford (Kelloway Township Building Department)** and obtain Kelloway Township's written Certificate of Final Approval, and **Halbray Community Solar LP (Imogen Strathe)** / **Tamsworth** must deliver the signed Certificate of Final Approval and revised as-built drawings to **Alleniom Commissioning (S. Rennick)** before `07/29/2025`.

2. **Collection Switchboard `CSB-1` Main Breaker (`2,000 A`) Undersized for 125% of Combined Continuous Inverter Output Current (`2,075.0 A`) (`Drawings E-201 Rev B` & `E-301 Rev B`)**
   - **What stands in the way:** All four skids (`4 × 415.0 A = 1,660.0 A` continuous) combine on `CSB-1`'s `2,500 A` bus and flow through the `CSB-1` main breaker to `T-1`. At 125%, the minimum required overcurrent device rating is **`1.25 × 1,660.0 A = 2,075.0 A`**, whereas `CSB-1` has a **`2,000 A` main breaker** (selected above `T-1`'s `1,804.22 A` [`1,804 A`] secondary full-load current without applying the 125% continuous factor to the `1,660.0 A` inverter output; an 80%-rated `2,000 A` breaker is limited to `1,600 A` continuous `< 1,660.0 A`).
   - **What it rests on:** **2023 NEC 690.8(A)(1)(a), 690.9(B), 705.28(A), 705.30(B), 215.2(A)(1)(a), 215.3, and 240.6(A)** (next standard rating above `2,075.0 A` is **`2,500 A`**).
   - **What must be done and by whom:** **Tamsworth Electrical Constructors Inc. (R. Vance)** must either replace the `CSB-1` main breaker with a **`2,500 A` main breaker** (`800 V, 65 kA`, matching the `2,500 A` bus and within the 250% secondary limit of **2023 NEC Table 450.3(A)** for `T-1`), OR provide manufacturer listing documentation demonstrating that the `CSB-1` switchboard assembly and its `2,000 A` main breaker are listed for **100% continuous operation** under **2023 NEC 690.9(B) Exception / 705.30(B) Exception / 215.3 Exception No. 1** and revise `Drawing E-201 Rev B`.

3. **Missing Multiple-Source Busbar Warning Label on `CSB-1` (and `ACP-1` to `ACP-4`) and Missing Power-Source Directory Plaque (`Drawing E-201 Rev B` — `CSB-1` & `Labels`)**
   - **What stands in the way:** On `CSB-1` (`2,500 A` bus), `2,000 A (main) + 2,075 A (125% PV) = 4,075 A > 3,000 A (120% of bus)`, so `CSB-1` relies on **NEC 705.12(B)(3)(3)** (`4 × 600 A + 30 A = 2,430 A ≤ 2,500 A` bus; similarly, each `ACP-1..4` has `2 × 225 A + 125 A = 575 A` on a `600 A` bus), but `Drawing E-201 Rev B` (`Labels`) omits the required permanent multiple-source busbar warning label and omits the permanent directory plaque at `POI`/`PSW-1` and `CSB-1` showing all electric power sources and disconnecting means.
   - **What it rests on:** **2023 NEC 705.12(B)(3)(3), 705.10, and 690.56(B)**.
   - **What must be done and by whom:** **Tamsworth Electrical Constructors Inc.** must install the **NEC 705.12(B)(3)(3)** multiple-source busbar warning labels on `CSB-1` and `ACP-1` to `ACP-4` and the **NEC 705.10 / 690.56(B)** directory plaques at `PSW-1`/`POI` and `CSB-1`, and update `Drawing E-201 Rev B`.

4. **Auxiliary Panelboard `AUX-1` & Tracker Controllers `TC-1 to TC-8`: Outdoor Enameled Steel Strut, 800 V Utilization Branch Circuits, Disconnecting Means, and Feeder Emergence (`Drawings E-201 Rev B` & `E-202 Rev B`)**
   - **What stands in the way:**
     - *(a) Outdoor Ferrous Strut Protected Solely by Enamel:* `Drawing E-202 Rev B` (`Mounting`) mounts outdoor `AUX-1` and `TC-1 to TC-8` on **`"1-5/8 in. steel strut, green enamel finish"`** at the array north edge.
     - *(b) 800 V Utilization Branch Circuits:* `AUX-1` (`800 V, 3-phase, 3-wire, 100 A main lugs`) is fed directly at `800 V` (`462 V` to ground) from a `30 A` breaker in `CSB-1` without an auxiliary step-down transformer, supplying eight 3-pole `15 A` branch circuits at **`800 V`** to permanently connected utilization equipment (`TC-1 to TC-8`).
     - *(c) Structure/Controller Disconnects & HDPE Feeder Riser:* `AUX-1` is a freestanding outdoor main-lug-only panelboard with **eight** branch breakers (exceeding the six-disconnect limit of **NEC 225.33(A)** for a separate structure supplied by a feeder under **225.31** without a local main disconnect); `AUX-1` (`beside TC-1`) is not in sight from distant controllers `TC-2 to TC-8` across blocks 2 to 8 and lacks specified lockable-open provisions per **NEC 430.102(A) & 110.25**; and the `AUX-1` feeder in `"1 in. HDPE duct in the collection trench"` lacks a specified rigid metal or Schedule 80 PVC riser stub-up where emerging above grade.
   - **What it rests on:** **2023 NEC 300.6(A), 210.6(D)(2), 210.6(C), 225.31, 225.33(A), 430.102(A), 110.25, 353.12(1)–(2), and 300.5(D)(1), (4)**.
   - **What must be done and by whom:** **Tamsworth Electrical Constructors Inc. (R. Vance)** must replace the green-enameled strut with hot-dip galvanized/stainless outdoor strut per **300.6(A)**; install an auxiliary step-down transformer (e.g., 800 V to 480 V or 240/120 V) and appropriately rated panelboard/controller inputs so utilization branch circuits do not exceed 600 V per **210.6(D)(2)**; provide a main disconnect at `AUX-1` per **225.31/225.33(A)** and in-sight or 110.25-lockable disconnects for `TC-1 to TC-8` per **430.102(A)**; install a rigid metal or Schedule 80 PVC riser stub-up where the `AUX-1` feeder emerges above grade per **353.12 & 300.5(D)**; and revise `Drawings E-201 Rev B` and `E-202 Rev B`.

5. **Tracker Row Slew-Drive Circuits Wired with Open `Type TC` Tray Cable Along Row Piles Outside a Raceway or Cable Tray (`Drawing E-203 Rev B` — `Wiring`)**
   - **What stands in the way:** `Drawing E-203 Rev B` (`Wiring`) specifies the 96 slew-drive circuits (`48 V dc, 0.25 kW`, 12 rows/block × 8 blocks) as **`"3 x 12 AWG Cu Type TC, from each block's controller along the row piles to each row's drive"`**—installed as open cable along the tracker row piles without a raceway or cable tray.
   - **What it rests on:** **2023 NEC 336.12(1)–(4) and 336.10(4), (7)**.
   - **What must be done and by whom:** **Tamsworth Electrical Constructors Inc. (R. Vance)** must enclose the slew-drive circuits in an NEC-compliant raceway or cable tray system (with sunlight-resistant cable) or replace the open `Type TC` runs with an NEC-permitted outdoor/underground wiring method (or AHJ-accepted protected `Type TC-ER`), and revise `Drawing E-203 Rev B`.

6. **Skid Inverter Output Circuits Using `Type USE-2` Conductors in Aboveground Rigid Steel Conduit on the Skid Frame (`Drawing E-301 Rev B` — `Inverter Circuits`)**
   - **What stands in the way:** `Drawing E-301 Rev B` (`Inverter Circuits`) specifies **`"3 x 1/C 4/0 AWG Cu, 2 kV Type USE-2"`** (`NG-XT-225` to `225 A` breaker) and **`"3 x 1/C 1 AWG Cu, 2 kV Type USE-2"`** (`NG-XT-125` to `125 A` breaker) in **`"Rigid steel conduit on the skid frame"`**—an entirely aboveground installation not emerging from the ground.
   - **What it rests on:** **2023 NEC 338.12(B)(2)**.
   - **What must be done and by whom:** **Tamsworth Electrical Constructors Inc. (R. Vance)** must either verify/document on `Drawing E-301 Rev B` that the installed conductors carry a dual **`Type RHW-2`** (or `XHHW-2`) rating/marking, OR replace the single-rated `2 kV Type USE-2` conductors in the skid-frame conduits with an NEC-permitted 2 kV conductor type such as **`2 kV Type RHW-2`**.

7. **`1,500 V dc` (Over 1,000 V) PV String Home Runs in Trench at `600 mm` (`24 in.`) Depth / Without Underground Raceway or Stub-Up Bushing (`Drawing E-301 Rev B` — `Pads`)**
   - **What stands in the way:** `Drawing E-301 Rev B` (`Pads`) routes `10 AWG PV wire, 2 kV` string home runs operating at **`maximum dc voltage 1,500 V`** (over `1,000 V`) from the array into `2 in. PVC-coated rigid steel conduit` pad stub-ups terminating **`600 mm` (`24 in.`) below grade to the trench**, with no underground raceway in the trench and no stub-up end bushings. Under **2023 NEC Table 300.50**, circuits over 1,000 V require **30 in. (`750 mm`)** minimum cover for direct-buried cables (or **18 in. [`450 mm`]** in nonmetallic raceway / **6 in. [`150 mm`]** in rigid metal conduit), plus end bushings where emerging from underground conduit stub-ups.
   - **What it rests on:** **2023 NEC 690.31(A), 690.31(B)(1), 690.31(C)(1), 300.50(A) & (C), and Table 300.50**.
   - **What must be done and by whom:** **Tamsworth Electrical Constructors Inc. (R. Vance)** must either enclose the `1,500 V dc` string home runs in the trench in a continuous underground raceway coupled to the 2 in. PVC-coated rigid steel stub-ups (`600 mm` cover satisfies **Table 300.50** for raceway), OR direct-bury direct-burial-listed PV wire at **`≥ 750 mm` (`30 in.`)** cover with **300.50(C)** bushings at the stub-up ends, apply **690.31(B)(1)** DC raceway markings, and revise `Drawing E-301 Rev B`.
