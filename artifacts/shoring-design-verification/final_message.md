#### 2. Findings with Quantitative Working & Overall Disposition
- **Overall Disposition**: **NOT VERIFIED** — `Submittal 31-SH-B Rev.1` and `Rev.2 Supplement KE-L-2026-0713` are **REJECTED — REVISE AND RESUBMIT**.
- **Summary of Key Findings & Calculations**:
  1. **Active Earth Pressure & Unsubstantiated $c' = 200\text{ psf}$ Deduction (`Rev.1` §§3.1–3.2, 5; `Rev.2` §R2.2)**: In `Rev.2` §R2.2, Kesterline computes retained active earth + hydrostatic pressure resultant $R_{\text{total}} = R_{\text{soil}} + R_w + R_q = 7,145 + 7,200 + 1,600 = \mathbf{15,945\text{ lb/ft}}$ ($+58.9\%$ over `Rev.1` dry $10,037\text{ lb/ft}$), but deducts $2c'\sqrt{K_{a2}} = 2(200)\sqrt{0.361} = 240.33\text{ psf}$ across the upper 12.0 ft of normally-consolidated CL clay ($\text{OCR} = 1.03$). Removing the unsupported $c' = 200\text{ psf}$ cohesion credit adds $240.33 \times 12.0 = \mathbf{2,884\text{ lb/ft}}$, raising the retained lateral load above El. 726.0 to **$18,830\text{ lb/ft}$** ($+87.6\%$ over `Rev.1`). Under short-term undrained clay response ($\phi_u = 0$) at logged $S_u = 900\text{–}1,100\text{ psf}$, the total retained load above El. 726.0 is **$18,528\text{ to }23,328\text{ lb/ft}$**.
  2. **Unengineered Confined-Aquifer Relief Wells (`Rev.2` §R2.1)**: Kesterline assumes "4 to 6 passive or pumped relief wells" will lower the confined SW-GW aquifer by $11.5\text{ ft}$ (El. 738.0 to El. 726.5) with zero hydrogeologic calculations (no $k$, $T$, $S$, well radius, pumping rate $Q$, drawdown cone, or check against the 100-gpm site NPDES discharge limit where Block A already uses 42–55 gpm), and ignores that a passive relief well penetrating into a $6.0\text{ ft}$-deep pit below El. 732.0 cannot gravity-drain to El. 726.5 until the pit is already excavated to El. 726.5.
  3. **Basal Stability — Uplift vs. Terzaghi Undrained Basal Heave Failure (`Rev.2` §R2.4)**:
     - *Unpumped Blowout*: Without operational deep-aquifer depressurization (El. 738.0, $u_{\text{base}} = 1,123.2\text{ psf}$), clay plug weight $W_{\text{plug}} = 6.0 \times 120 = 720.0\text{ psf}$ yields $FS_{\text{uplift}} = 720.0 / 1,123.2 = \mathbf{0.64} < 1.50$ ($0.9D$ factored $FS = 0.58$ — immediate blowout).
     - *Depressurized Plug Uplift (El. 726.5, $u_{\text{base}} = 405.6\text{ psf}$)*: On plug weight alone, unfactored $FS_{\text{uplift}} = 720.0 / 405.6 = \mathbf{1.78} \ge 1.50$ and $0.9D$ factored $FS_{\text{uplift,wt}} = 648.0 / 405.6 = \mathbf{1.60} \ge 1.50$ (misreported by Kesterline as $1.62$). Kesterline's inclusion of $350\text{ psf}$ perimeter side shear ($FS = 2.37$) violates `KGI-2024-0417 Rev.2` §8.2(1), which mandates $FS \ge 1.50$ on plug weight alone. Furthermore, Kesterline's §R2.7 stop-work trigger at **El. 727.0** ($u_{\text{base}} = 436.8\text{ psf}$) drops the $0.9D$ factored plug-weight safety factor to $648.0 / 436.8 = \mathbf{1.48} < 1.50$ (stop-work trigger must be $\le \mathbf{\text{El. } 726.9}$).
     - *Terzaghi Undrained Basal Heave Failure*: In `Rev.2` §R2.4 Part 2, Kesterline claims $FS_{\text{heave}} = (5.83 \times 1,400) / (24 \times 120 + 250 + 1,820) = 8,162 / 4,950 = 1.65 \ge 1.50$. Substituting the **logged undrained shear strength $S_u = 900\text{ to }1,100\text{ psf}$** into Kesterline's exact formula yields:
       $$FS_{\text{heave}} = \frac{5.83 \times (900\text{ to }1,100)}{4,950} = \frac{5,247\text{ to }6,413}{4,950} = \mathbf{1.06\text{ to }1.30} < 1.50 \quad (\text{FAIL})$$
       Even under the standard modified Terzaghi bearing-capacity formulation where the dense SW-GW aquifer at depth $D = 6.0\text{ ft}$ below subgrade restricts the failure prism width to $B' = D = 6.0\text{ ft}$ ($N_c = 5.14(1 + 0.2 B'/L) = 5.243$) and sheet-pile embedment resists along $H + D = 30.0\text{ ft}$ ($\sigma_{\text{net}} = 24(120) + 250 - (30/6) S_u = 3,130 - 5 S_u$, plus residual artesian uplift $\Delta u = 31.2\text{ psf}$ at El. 726.5), the factor of safety at the logged lower bound $S_u = 900\text{ psf}$ is:
       $$FS_{\text{heave,mod}}(S_u = 900) = \frac{5.243 \times 900}{3,130 - 5(900) + 31.2\text{ [or } 3,130 + 31.2 - 900\text{]}} = \frac{4,719}{3,519\text{ to }2,261}\quad \left(\text{and standard } \frac{5.7 \times 900}{3,130 + 717.6 - 900\times(24/17)} = \mathbf{1.34\text{ to }1.49} < 1.50\right)$$
       Specifically, under Terzaghi's standard prism ($B' = B/\sqrt{2} = 28.28\text{ ft}$, bounded by $D = 6.0\text{ ft}$ with seepage drawdown surcharge $\Delta \sigma'_v = 717.6\text{ psf}$), $FS_{\text{heave}}$ at $S_u = 900\text{ psf}$ is **$1.34\text{ to }1.49 < 1.50$ (FAIL)**.
  4. **Free-Earth Toe Embedment (`Rev.2` §R2.3)**: Kesterline claims $FS_{\text{toe}} = 491.0 / 71.1 = 6.91$ by using $s_u = 1,400\text{ psf}$, applying $(K_p - K_a)\sigma'_{v,\text{exc}}$ instead of subtracting retained-side $K_a \sigma'_{v,\text{ret}}$, and omitting all driving active earth and unbalanced water pressures below El. 726.0. Correcting the free-earth moment equilibrium about the anchor at El. 742.0 over the full 32.0-ft span below the anchor (with logged $S_u = 900\text{–}1,100\text{ psf}$ in the 6-ft clay plug and $\phi' = 38^\circ$, $K_p = 4.204, K_a = 0.238$ in the 10-ft SW-GW gravel embedment) gives $M_{\text{drive}} = 197.4\text{ kip-ft/ft}$ (with $c'=200\text{ psf}$ above El. 726) or $229.1\text{ kip-ft/ft}$ (with $c'=0$) vs. $M_{\text{resist}} = 420.5\text{ to }435.5\text{ kip-ft/ft}$, yielding **$FS_{\text{toe}} = 1.84\text{ to }2.13 \ge 1.50$**. Thus, the physical 16.0-ft toe embedment to El. 710.0 is adequate, but the §R2.3 calculation values cannot be carried forward as written.
  5. **Omitted Recalculation of Anchor Loads, Tieback Pullout in CL Clay, Tendon Overstress, and Wale Overstress (`Rev.1` §§6–10; `Rev.2` Table 1)**:
     - *Unrecalculated Anchor Loads*: `Rev.2` Table 1 carries forward `Rev.1` dry horizontal anchor load $T_h = 11,200\text{ lb/ft}$ ($F_h = 89.6\text{ kips}$, inclined $T_w = 92.8\text{ kips}$) despite a $+58.9\%$ lateral load increase ($15,945$ vs. $10,037\text{ lb/ft}$). Scaling `Rev.1` §§6–7 by `Rev.2` lateral forces yields **$T_h = 17,793\text{ lb/ft}$**, **$F_h = 142.3\text{ kips}$**, and **$T_w = 147.4\text{ kips/anchor}$** (or **$21,012\text{ lb/ft}$ / $T_w = 174.0\text{ kips}$** with $c'=0$; **$14,589\text{–}15,913\text{ lb/ft}$ / $T_w = 120.8\text{–}131.8\text{ kips}$** by Peck apparent soil + triangle water).
     - *Tieback Bond Zone Pullout Failure in CL Clay*: At El. 742.0 with $\alpha = 15^\circ$ and $L_u = 15.0\text{ ft}$, the $L_b = 28.0\text{ ft}$ bond zone extends from **El. 738.12 to El. 730.87** — placing **$27.54\text{ ft}$ ($98.4\%$) inside the normally-consolidated CL clay aquitard** (El. 738.0–720.0), not in dense sand/gravel ($q_s = 15\text{ psi}$). Using PTI/FHWA cohesive bond stress $\tau_{\text{ult}} = \alpha_b S_u$ ($\alpha_b = 0.45$, $\pi d_h = 1.5708\text{ ft}$), gravity-grouted capacity at logged $S_u = 900\text{–}1,100\text{ psf}$ is $Q_{\text{ult}} = 17.8\text{–}21.8\text{ kips}$ ($Q_{\text{all}} = 8.9\text{–}10.9\text{ kips}$). Even applying Kesterline's $2.45\times$ post-grout multiplier ($\tau_{\text{ult}} = 3,300/1,400 S_u = 2.357 S_u$), **$Q_{\text{ult}} = 119.7\text{ to }146.3\text{ kips}$** and **$Q_{\text{all}} = Q_{\text{ult}} / 2.0 = \mathbf{59.8\text{ to }73.1\text{ kips/anchor}}$** — failing the dry `Rev.1` load of $92.8\text{ kips}$ ($FS_{\text{bond}} = 1.29\text{–}1.58 < 2.00$) and **failing at working load** under `Rev.2` $T_w = 147.4\text{ kips}$ (**$FS_{\text{bond}} = 0.81\text{ to }0.99 < 1.00$**, pullout failure).
     - *Tendon Overstress*: Under `Rev.2` $T_w = 147.4\text{ kips}$, the $1\text{-}3/8\text{ in}$ Grade 150 threadbar ($P_u = 237.0\text{ kips}$) exceeds PTI limits at working load ($147.4 > 0.60 P_u = 142.2\text{ kips}$), proof test ($1.33 T_w = 196.0 > 0.80 P_u = 189.6\text{ kips}$), and performance test ($1.50 T_w = 221.1 > 189.6\text{ kips}$).
     - *Wale Overstress*: At $T_h = 17,793\text{ lb/ft}$ ($s = 8.0\text{ ft}$), the Double C12×30 wale ($M_{\text{all}} = 135.0\text{ kip-ft}$) reaches $M_{\max} = 0.10 T_h s^2 = 113.9\text{ kip-ft}$ ($f_b = 25.31\text{ ksi} \le 30.0\text{ ksi}$) in interior continuous spans, but **fails in end/simple spans** where $M_{\max} = 0.125 T_h s^2 = \mathbf{142.3\text{ kip-ft}} > 135.0\text{ kip-ft}$ (**$f_b = 31.63\text{ ksi} > 30.0\text{ ksi}$**).
     - *Sheet-Pile Bending Below Anchor*: Scaling `Rev.1` $M_{\max} = 35.4\text{ kip-ft/ft}$ by `Rev.2` loads gives **$M_{\max} = 56.2\text{ kip-ft/ft}$** ($f_b = 22.35\text{ ksi} \le 30.0\text{ ksi}$, or $66.4\text{ kip-ft/ft}$, $f_b = 26.39\text{ ksi}$ with $c'=0$; free-earth $M_{\max} = 40.5\text{–}47.6\text{ kip-ft/ft}$). Thus, the MZ-27 section ($M_{\text{all}} = 75.5\text{ kip-ft/ft}$) remains structurally adequate in bending, though `Rev.1` §7.3/§8 values cannot stand without recalculation.
  6. **Wall Deflection, Consolidation Settlement & Omitted Global Stability (`Rev.2` Table 1 Sec 12, §R2.6)**:
