#### 2. Matters on the Record Standing in the Way of Permission to Operate

1. **Obstacle 1 — Utility RTU Station AC Service (`PP-1 Circuit 1`) and `SWBD-PV` Enclosure Heaters (`PP-1 Circuit 2`) Fed from `SWBD-PV` on the Customer/DER Side of the Open POI Recloser**
   * **What it is:** Under `PXA-2023-0629_Corwyn_Equipment_Schedule.xlsx` (`Drawing E-101 Rev C`, `06/24/2025`, rows 2–6), auxiliary panel `PP-1` (`120/240 V`, `100 A` bus, `70 A` main breaker) supplies **`Circuit 1`** (the dedicated `20 A`, `120 V` circuit to the utility RTU enclosure on the POI pole through its `1.5 kVA` online UPS rated for `5.5 h` at the RTU load) and **`Circuit 2`** (`20 A` `SWBD-PV` enclosure heaters). However, `PP-1` is fed by auxiliary transformer `TX-1` (`15 kVA`, `480 V to 120/240 V`), which is fed from a `40 A` two-pole breaker in `SWBD-PV` (`480Y/277 V`) on the customer/DER side of step-up transformer `T-1` (`750 kVA`), primary switch `PSW-1`, and the Company's POI recloser. During the witness test on `07/08/2025`, Distribution Operations closed the POI recloser locally at `08:10` under switching order `DO-2025-0677`, energizing `T-1`, `SWBD-PV`, `TX-1`, and `PP-1`, and opened it locally at `15:35` (`WT-0629-01`, p. 1). Because `ISA §6.1` mandates that *"At all other times before permission to operate the POI recloser is kept open,"* `PP-1` lost AC power at `15:35` on `07/08/2025`, the `5.5 h` RTU UPS battery depleted around `21:05` on `07/08/2025`, and the utility RTU lost power and SCADA telemetry. Post-PTO, any trip/lockout of the POI recloser (`DPS-07 §5.1(b)`, `ISA §8.4`), Company opening (`ISA §8.3`), or clearance opening of `PSW-1` or `SWBD-PV` main (`ISA §8.5`) would similarly de-energize `PP-1`, shutting down the RTU after 5.5 hours (and de-energizing the `SWBD-PV` space heaters while `SWBD-PV` is cold).
   * **What it rests on:** `Drawing E-101 Rev C` (rows 2–6); `Drawing E-401 Rev C` (`Cabinet` row 2); `WT-0629-01` (p. 1); `ISA-Corwyn-Office-Complex §§4.1, 5.2, 6.1, 6.2, 6.3, 7.2, 8.3–8.5, Exhibits A & B`; `TCS-02 §§3.1, 3.4, 4.3, 5(a)–(c)`; `DIS-01 Rev 4 §5 Table 5-1 & §6`; `DPS-07 Rev 2 §§4.1(c), 4.2(4), 5.1(b), 6.3`.
   * **What has to be done and by whom:** **Corwyn Realty Partners** (`Nadia Ferreira-Holt, Director of Property Operations`) and **Brantley Electric Company (Alleron Electrical Contractor Lic. #EC-12306)** (`K. Osei, Electrical Engineer`) must re-feed the utility RTU station AC service (or `PP-1` / `TX-1` so both `PP-1 Circuit 1` and `PP-1 Circuit 2` remain energized when `SWBD-PV` is de-energized) from an unswitched, continuously energized building house-power panel on the office-complex load service (such as `office building panel LP-2` in the parking-structure electrical room, which already feeds `DAS-1` on circuit 14 per `Drawing E-401 Rev C`), issue revised `Drawing E-101 Rev D`, and obtain supplemental electrical inspection approval from the **City of Wrenford Department of Inspections** (`Dolores Achebe, Chief Electrical Inspector`) under Permit `WEP-25-0317` (`ISA §§4.2, 6.2`).
   * **Can it be done before `08/04/2025`?** **Yes** (within the 21 calendar days / 15 business days available).

2. **Obstacle 2 — Inverter Internal Control Power (`INV-C1` to `INV-C5`) Derived from 480 V AC Output Terminals While the POI Recloser Is Kept Open Before PTO (`COM-RTU` Telemetry Deadlock)**
   * **What it is:** Under `Drawing E-401 Rev C` (`Links` row 7), the utility RTU polls `INV-C1` to `INV-C5` over `COM-RTU` (`RS-485 port 2, one multi-drop bus`) for per-inverter AC power (`Gross generation (per inverter block)`) and status (`Inverter/PCS block status`) required by `TCS-02 §6.1`. Under `Drawing E-101 Rev C` (row 7), each of the `5 x Vexlar Energy VX-140C (140 kW ac, 480 V, 3-phase, UL 1741-SB, firmware family 4.2, build 4.2.7)` inverters takes its `"control power internal, from the inverter's own ac output terminals."` Even after the RTU enclosure is re-fed from building house power, while the POI recloser is kept open before PTO under `ISA §6.1` (which contains no pre-PTO backfeed provision, unlike `ISA-Halbray-Field-Site-3`), `T-1` and `SWBD-PV` are de-energized, leaving `INV-C1` to `INV-C5` without 480 V AC at their output terminals to power their internal control/RS-485 electronics. Under `TCS-02 §6.3`, the RTU must report those unreachable per-inverter points with the `communications-lost` quality flag, preventing satisfaction of `TCS-02 §5(c)` (which requires all `§6` points to be valid and not flagged bad-quality at the SCADA operator display at the moment PTO is issued).
   * **What it rests on:** `Drawing E-101 Rev C` (row 7); `Drawing E-401 Rev C` (`Links` row 7); `ISA-Corwyn-Office-Complex §§6.1, 6.2, 7.2, 12.1`; `TCS-02 §§3.3(a), 5(c), 6.1, 6.3`.
   * **What has to be done and by whom:** Either **(Path A)** **Corwyn Realty Partners** (`Nadia Ferreira-Holt`) and **Alleniom Utilities** (`Dana Whitcomb, VP Distribution Operations`, coordinated by the **DER Program Office** — `K. Ondras` / `S. Rennick, Commissioning, DER Program Office`) execute a brief written change order under `ISA §12.1` authorizing pre-PTO backfeed of `T-1` and `SWBD-PV` under a **Distribution Operations** switching order (with `INV-C1` to `INV-C5` disabled from parallel export / in standby) so 480 V AC energizes inverter control power and `COM-RTU` reports valid standby status and `0 kW` output prior to PTO issuance; **or (Path B)** **Corwyn Realty Partners** and **Brantley Electric Company** (`K. Osei`), with **Vexlar Energy** (`T. Lindgren`), supply continuous auxiliary control power from house power to the inverters' communications cards so `COM-RTU` stays online while `SWBD-PV` is open.
   * **Can it be done before `08/04/2025`?** **Yes.**

3. **Obstacle 3 — Per-Inverter SCADA Polling Cadence on `Drawing E-401 Rev C` (`5 min` vs. `TCS-02 §6.2` `≤ 4 s` Cadence), `Drawing E-301` Schedule Clarification, and Fresh Day-of-PTO SOC Live-Telemetry Confirmation (`TCS-02 §5(c)`)**
   * **What it is:** (a) `Drawing E-401 Rev C` (`Header` and `Points` rows 2–3) covers both `DAS-1` and `COM-RTU` (`ISA §7.2`) but lists a `"5 min"` interval for `"AC power, energy, voltage and current per inverter"` and `"On change"` for `"Inverter status and fault codes"`, whereas `TCS-02 §§6.1–6.2` require analog `Gross generation (per inverter block) (kW)` to update to SCADA at **`≤ 4 s`** intervals (with a `≤ 60 s` Class 0 integrity poll) and status points to report on change with a **`≤ 60 s`** integrity poll (`5 min` applies only to cumulative energy registers `kWh` and customer historian `DAS-1`). (b) `Drawing E-301 Rev C` (`PV Output Circuits` row 2) should explicitly clarify `"in individual 2 in. rigid steel conduits (one per combiner)"` down the inverter column to match the plural `"Column risers"` in `Drawing E-201 Rev C` and the un-derated `236.6 A` ampacity (`NEC Ch. 9 Table 1` & `310.15(C)(1)`). (c) The SOC live-telemetry sign-off on Commissioning Checklist `CX-CW-01 Rev 2 (07/14/2025)` was recorded on `07/08/2025` during the witness-test switching window (`08:10 to 15:35`, two days before the `07/10/2025` PTO request and before the POI recloser opened at `15:35`), whereas `TCS-02 §5(c)` requires live telemetry presenting all `§6` points as valid **at the moment PTO is issued**.
   * **What it rests on:** `Drawing E-401 Rev C`; `Drawing E-301 Rev C`; `CX-CW-01 Rev 2 (07/14/2025)`; `TCS-02 §§3.3(a), 5(b)–(c), 6.1, 6.2`; `ISA §§6.2, 6.3, 7.2`; `DPS-07 §6.3`.
   * **What has to be done and by whom:** **Corwyn Realty Partners** and **Brantley Electric Company** (`K. Osei`) issue `Drawing E-401 Rev D` (and `Drawing E-301 Rev D` clarification) and confirm with **SCADA/EMS Engineering** (`Marcus Deele`) that `COM-RTU` (`RS-485 port 2`) updates at the `TCS-02 §6.2` cadence (`≤ 4 s` analog / `≤ 60 s` integrity poll); once Obstacles 1 and 2 are resolved, **SCADA/EMS Engineering** (`Marcus Deele`) verifies all `TCS-02 §6` points valid at SCADA, **Commissioning Coordinator** (`S. Rennick, Commissioning, DER Program Office`) updates `CX-CW-01`, and the **SOC Shift Supervisor** (`Lena Kovalchuk`) confirms live telemetry at the moment PTO issues on `08/04/2025` (`TCS-02 §5(c)`) and observes first parallel operation under active SCADA supervision (`ISA §6.3`, `DPS-07 §6.3`).
   * **Can it be done before `08/04/2025`?** **Yes.**
